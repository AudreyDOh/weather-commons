# Sources and interpretive scope

- **GOES imagery in the shared 3D Earth.** [NOAA/NESDIS/STAR GOES-19 full-disk products](https://www.star.nesdis.noaa.gov/GOES/fulldisk.php?sat=G19). Four twelve-frame sequences cover 3 September 2026, 04:10–06:00 UTC: GeoColor, AirMass RGB, Sandwich RGB and band 16. A derived-motion-wind still has its own 05:00 UTC label. Original JPEGs, exact URLs, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are in `public/data/archive/goes/manifest.json`. Reproduce while upstream retains the files with `pnpm data:goes --end=20262460600`. The retrieval script selects a common consecutive window if no end is supplied. No raw ABI radiances or independent wind vectors are ingested.
- **Interpretation.** [Air Mass RGB quick guide](https://www.star.nesdis.noaa.gov/GOES/documents/QuickGuide_GOESR_AirMassRGB_final.pdf) and [derived motion winds quick guide](https://www.star.nesdis.noaa.gov/GOES/documents/QuickGuide_BaselineDerivedMotionWinds.pdf). Colour encodes product-specific relationships rather than a universal legend. CO₂ band 16 is a 13.3 µm infrared channel, not an emissions inventory. Source colours and embedded overlays are retained; published JPEGs use approximate fixed-grid registration on the globe. The pilot adds no lighting to these source images and does not infer volumetric clouds or altitude from their apparent depth.

- **Current typography.** The active UI uses regular (400) bundled [Libre Franklin](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/librefranklin), served locally with its SIL OFL license in `public/fonts/OFL-LibreFranklin.txt`.
- **Temporal display.** Daily cloud images may visually dissolve for 1.4 seconds; both source dates are shown while playing. Spatial feathering and temporal dissolves are aesthetic processing, not new observations. For higher cadence, [NOAA GOES full-disk imagery](https://www.goes.noaa.gov/fulldisk.php?sat=G19&src=nav) updates every 10 minutes. The dated GOES archive is now integrated with the 3D globe.

- **MODIS cloud isolation and assumed height.** The pilot's cloud view is a display heuristic: brightness threshold plus colour neutrality, preserving the source alpha mask. It is not NASA's scientific cloud mask. The uniform shell height is assumed, with radius `6371 km + assumedHeightKm × exaggeration`; no cloud-top height is inferred from RGB. Scientific alternatives: [NASA MOD35 cloud mask](https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/mod35.php) uses visible and infrared tests; [NASA MOD06 cloud product](https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/mod06.php) supplies retrieved cloud properties. Neither product has been downloaded or integrated in this milestone.

- **Animated observation archive.** NASA GIBS, Terra/MODIS Corrected Reflectance True Color: https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/python-usage/ . Six daily mosaics, 24–29 September 2024, retrieved as transparent EPSG:4326 WMS images at 2048 × 1024. Exact requests, retrieval timestamp and SHA-256 hashes are in `public/data/imagery/manifest.json`; reproduce with `pnpm data:imagery`. Files are unmodified service responses; monochrome shading and opacity are display operations. The image sequence includes clouds and surface together, with missing-coverage gaps. It does not supply cloud heights, drive the tracer model, or describe 1966 conditions. We acknowledge imagery provided by NASA Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of EOSDIS.

- **Project Popeye memorandum.** Foy D. Kohler to Dean Rusk, 13 January 1967, FRUS 1964–1968, volume XXVIII, document 274. https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v28/d274 . Records an October 1966 test and proposes military use; contains official claims and anticipated risks, not independent attribution of rainfall or civilian harm. Historical site positions in this pilot are contextual, not reconstructed missions.
- **Civilian land and infrastructure.** UN Lao PDR, “Unexploded Ordnance, an Obstacle to Development,” 4 April 2018. https://laopdr.un.org/en/14539-unexploded-ordnance-obstacle-development . Documents effects of UXO on agriculture, construction and livelihoods. These are bombing legacies and are kept separate from weather-modification claims.
- **ERA5.** Hersbach et al., ERA5 hourly data on single levels from 1940 to present. https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.adbb2d47 . Retrieved through Open-Meteo's historical API: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api . Processed sample metadata and precise request URLs: public/data/popeye-archive.json. Original responses and hashes: public/data/archive. Reanalysis is a model-observation synthesis; this pilot uses a coarsened, stationary wind snapshot. Open-Meteo attribution: https://doi.org/10.5281/ZENODO.7970649 . Data served by Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0.
- **HYSPLIT.** NOAA Air Resources Laboratory. https://www.arl.noaa.gov/hysplit/ . Established transport and dispersion model cited as a methodological reference. HYSPLIT itself is not installed or executed here.
- **Settlements.** Natural Earth 1:10m populated places, simple GeoJSON. https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson . The regional subset contains 38 points, not all settlements. No population counts or historical building footprints are used. Names and positions follow this gazetteer.
- **Rivers.** Natural Earth 1:50m rivers and lake centre lines. https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_rivers_lake_centerlines.geojson . The subset contains the Mekong, not a complete tributary or drainage model.
- **Boundaries.** Natural Earth Admin-0 land boundary lines, 1:50m. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-boundary-lines-2/ . Generalized reference geometry; disputes follow the source representation. Natural Earth data are public domain.
- **Earth image.** NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, January 2004 composite. https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/ . 5,400 × 2,700; source colour retained with interpretive relief lighting. This is not 1966 imagery or a live satellite feed.
- **Earlier typeface, retained asset only.** STIX Two Text, SIL Open Font License. Local font and license in public/fonts. https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/stixtwotext .

- **Cloud-top retrievals.** [NOAA ABI ACHA](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ncdc%3AC01505), GOES-R Algorithm Working Group and GOES-R Series Program Office, DOI [10.7289/V5HX19ZQ](https://doi.org/10.7289/V5HX19ZQ), accessed 3 September 2026. Exact GOES-19 ABI-L2-ACHAF NetCDF URLs and scan windows are in `public/data/archive/cloud-height/manifest.json`. The selected 1086 × 1086 stream is about 10 km at nadir, displayed every second source sample. This is not ACHA2KM. Packed HT is decoded, DQF=0 retained, missing pixels excluded. Source fixed-grid/ellipsoid navigation uses origin 75°W. The top surface is triangulated for display with no missing-data bridges or >2.5 km height jumps. Radial display height approximates geopotential height; no cloud-parallax correction, cloud base, density or rainfall model is supplied.
- **Land and seafloor.** [NOAA ETOPO1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model/etopo1), 2009 ice-surface global relief compilation. Original float32 TIFF export, ArcGIS service metadata and derived signed-metre grid are archived in `public/data/archive/earth/`. A bilinearly resampled 2048 × 1024 global overview supplies displaced geometry. Colour shading and vertical exaggeration are interpretive. It is not local terrain accuracy.
- **Built fabric.** © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL. Original Overpass response and 522 derived building outlines are archived with exact query, retrieval date and hashes. Height tags are preferred; levels use an assumed 3 m and missing tags an explicit 9 m fallback. Heights are bounded to 3–600 m. Geometry is a flat-roof extrusion on a local sea-level datum, not a historical or surveyed city reconstruction. The source record is available by clicking each building.
- **Aerial texture.** [USGS NAIP image service](https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPImagery/ImageServer). Archived 2048 × 1536 export, service metadata and catalogue query cover Lower Manhattan. The catalogue contains 2019/2023 acquisitions and overview entries; the exported mosaic is not assigned a single date. Ground and flat roofs use this image; walls use neutral display shading, not invented photographic facades. Image and footprint dates are not synchronized.
