Narrate

This is a preparation page, not a completed case study. You can see how a simple movement rule becomes an image. The next step is to choose a case and gather sources before making claims about weather or its effects.

Sources and limits

  • Synthetic model: the Realtime Weather starter template, Realtime Urbanism Lab, copied on 2 November 2026. The code is MIT-licensed; original text is CC BY 4.0. No external weather dataset is loaded.
  • Rule: 1,500 particles move 1.2 pixels east and 0.2 pixels north per animation frame, plus random spread of up to 0.3 pixels per axis. Particles wrap around the canvas edges. These are screen units, not metres or real elapsed time.
  • Changes: the working title, author, research status and explanatory text were added. The template’s movement rule is unchanged.
  • Research sources: to be selected after the case is chosen. Their dates, licences and limitations will be recorded here.

What this sketch cannot show: weather at any real place or time, the effect of an intervention, or impacts on people or ecosystems. The animation is an illustrative placeholder.
